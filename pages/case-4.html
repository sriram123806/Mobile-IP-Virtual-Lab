Case Study 04

Decapsulation Failure at Foreign Agent

Mobile IP Diagnostic Procedure & Decision Flowchart

← Back to Learning Page

Problem Description

Tunneled packets arrive at the FA but the original datagram is never delivered to the MN.

Possible Causes

  • Protocol 4 (IP-in-IP) packets blocked or not processed
  • Outer destination is not the FA care-of address
  • Inner destination (MN home address) has no visitor entry
  • Corrupted packet (bad outer checksum)

Diagnostic Procedure

  1. Capture traffic at the FA for protocol 4
  2. Check outer destination equals the FA care-of address
  3. Check the FA visitor list for the MN
  4. Verify header checksums

Corrective Actions

  • Allow IP-in-IP traffic
  • Correct CoA and visitor entry
Expected Outcome:

The FA strips the outer header, recovers the inner datagram and delivers it to the MN link.

Decision & Troubleshooting Flowchart

Evaluate each condition sequentially. Follow Green branches when checks pass, or execute the Red corrective actions when a check fails.

DIAGNOSTIC START Decapsulation Failure at Foreign Agent
↓
CHECK 1 Do protocol 4 packets reach the FA (not filtered)?
NO → Permit IP protocol 4 through firewalls and ACLs.
YES ↓ Condition verified. Proceed to next step.
↓
CHECK 2 Is the outer destination address the FA care-of address?
NO → Correct the care-of address registered at the HA.
YES ↓ Condition verified. Proceed to next step.
↓
CHECK 3 Is the MN in the FA visitor list?
NO → Repeat registration so the FA creates a visitor entry.
YES ↓ Condition verified. Proceed to next step.
↓
CHECK 4 Is the outer header checksum valid?
NO → Inspect the link for corruption; the FA must discard bad packets and the sender retransmits.
YES ↓ Condition verified. Proceed to next step.
↓
FINAL RESOLUTION The FA strips the outer header, recovers the inner datagram and delivers it to the MN link.
