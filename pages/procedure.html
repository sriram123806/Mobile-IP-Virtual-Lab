Procedure

Follow these 14 steps to operate the simulation. Steps 1–9 are setup steps; steps 10–14 describe what you will observe while the packet travels.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1

    🖥️Step 1: Open the Simulation page

    Click SIMULATION in the top navigation menu to open the lab.

  2. 2

    📍Step 2: Select the Correspondent Node (CN) location

    From the CN dropdown, choose a city in Tamil Nadu where the sender is located.

  3. 3

    🏠Step 3: Select the Home Agent (HA) location

    Choose the city representing the Mobile Node's home network. This must be different from CN.

  4. 4

    🌐Step 4: Select the Foreign Agent (FA) location

    Choose a third city representing the visited network where the Mobile Node currently is. Must differ from both CN and HA.

  5. 5

    ✅Step 5: Confirm the selected locations

    Click OK next to the location selectors. The Tamil Nadu map will show your CN, HA and FA with the tunnel highlighted.

  6. 6

    ⌨️Step 6: Enter a message

    Type an ASCII message (up to 40 characters) in the message input field — for example, Hello Mobile IP.

  7. 7

    🔘Step 7: Press OK to convert the message

    The message is converted into 8-bit ASCII binary. Each character shows its binary code and ASCII decimal value.

  8. 8

    🔍Step 8: Check the binary representation

    Each character appears as 8 bits in the bitstream display. Verify that it matches your original message.

  9. 9

    ▶️Step 9: Start the simulation

    Press START to begin the animated packet-delivery sequence. Use PAUSE and RESUME to control playback, and RESET to run again.

  10. 10

    📦Step 10: Observe packet generation at the CN

    The CN creates a packet addressed to the Mobile Node's home address. The packet panel shows the inner IP header.

  11. 11

    🛡️Step 11: Observe interception and encapsulation at the HA

    The HA intercepts the packet and adds an outer IP header addressed to the FA (care-of address). The packet panel shows both headers.

  12. 12

    🚇Step 12: Observe the Forward Tunnel

    Watch the encapsulated packet animate from the HA city to the FA city along the tunnel on the Tamil Nadu map.

  13. 13

    📤Step 13: Observe decapsulation at the FA

    The FA removes the outer header and recovers the original datagram. The outer header disappears from the packet panel.

  14. 14

    📱Step 14: Observe final delivery to the Mobile Node

    The Mobile Node receives the original message. A success message confirms delivery and the waveform turns cyan for all bits.
