PROCEDURE

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1

    Open the Simulation Page

    Navigate to the Simulation tab from the main navigation bar.

  2. 2

    Select Correspondent Node Location

    Choose the CN location from the dropdown selector.

  3. 3

    Select Home Agent Location

    Choose the HA location from the dropdown selector.

  4. 4

    Select Foreign Agent Location

    Choose the FA location from the dropdown selector.

  5. 5

    Confirm the Selected Locations

    Verify all three locations appear on the map.

  6. 6

    Enter a Message

    Type your message in the input field provided.

  7. 7

    Press OK to Convert

    Click OK to convert the message into 8-bit ASCII binary.

  8. 8

    Check Binary Representation

    Observe the binary output displayed on screen.

  9. 9

    Start the Simulation

    Click the START button to begin packet animation.

  10. 10

    Observe Packet Generation at CN

    Watch as the packet is generated at the Correspondent Node.

  11. 11

    Observe Packet Interception at HA

    See the Home Agent intercept and capture the packet.

  12. 12

    Observe Forward Tunnel Transmission

    Watch the encapsulated packet travel through the tunnel.

  13. 13

    Observe Decapsulation at FA

    See the Foreign Agent strip the outer IP header.

  14. 14

    Observe Final Delivery to Mobile Node

    Watch the original packet delivered to the Mobile Node.
