Packet Dash

The Mobile IP Tunneling Adventure

Help the glowing data packet travel from the Correspondent Node to the Mobile Node. Discover how the Home Agent and Foreign Agent deliver packets using encapsulation and tunneling!

📱 Best played with your device in landscape

How to Play

CONTROLS

  • ← / → : Move the packet.
  • SPACE : Jump over obstacles.
  • P : Pause or resume the game.
  • M : Mute or unmute sound.
  • On phones, use the on-screen buttons.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Help the blue packet travel through all five stages.
  • Collect green data bits to increase your score.
  • Avoid pink obstacles and interference.
  • You have three lives.
  • Each obstacle collision costs one life.
  • After a collision, the packet temporarily slows down and then recovers its normal speed.
  • At the Home Agent, watch how the green outer IP header wraps around the original packet. This is encapsulation.
  • During the tunnel journey, the encapsulated packet travels towards the Foreign Agent.
  • At the Foreign Agent, the green outer header is removed. This is decapsulation.
  • Complete the journey and deliver the original packet to the Mobile Node.
